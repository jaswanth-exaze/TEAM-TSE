## A practical reading order

1. Read *Clean Code*, Chapter 9, for test structure and clarity.
2. Read *Test-Driven Development: By Example* for the small-step workflow.
3. Read Meszaros for the vocabulary of test doubles: dummy, stub, fake, spy, and mock.
4. Compare the test-pyramid and TDD-style discussions with the way your own team works.
