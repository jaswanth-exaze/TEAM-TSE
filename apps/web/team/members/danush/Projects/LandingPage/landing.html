This Movie Overview

Varanasi is a highly anticipated global action-adventure and sci-fi film directed by the visionary filmmaker S.S. Rajamouli.
The story follows Rudhra, an archaeologist and dedicated Shiva devotee played by Mahesh Babu, who must embark on a high-stakes, time-traveling quest to stop a catastrophic asteroid from destroying the holy city of Varanasi.
Alongside a skilled historian named Mandakini (Priyanka Chopra Jonas), Rudhra must travel through different historical eras to locate a lost cosmic artifact before a brilliant but dangerous tech-genius named Kumbha (Prithviraj Sukumaran) can use it for global domination.
Written by V. Vijayendra Prasad and featuring music by M.M. Keeravaani, this massive cinematic epic is scheduled for a grand theatrical release on April 7, 2027, across 120 countries and in 50 different languages.
